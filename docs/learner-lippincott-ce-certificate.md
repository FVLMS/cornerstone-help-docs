# Lippincott Professional Development CE Certificate

Use this guide to download a continuing education certificate from Lippincott Professional Development after completing an eligible course.

## Before You Begin

- Complete the Lippincott lesson, pass the posttest, and complete the CE course evaluation first.
- The Fairview LMS transcript provides the Fairview completion certificate.
- The Lippincott CE certificate is stored in Lippincott and requires a personal Lippincott Professional Development account.

## Steps

1. After the course evaluation is complete, select **Get CE Certificate** in the alert window.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-03.png" alt="Lippincott alert with Get CE Certificate option">

2. If you already have a Lippincott Professional Development profile, sign in with your username and personal password.
3. If you do not remember your password, use **Forgot Password?**.
4. If you do not have a profile, select **Register with Lippincott Professional Development CE**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-06.png" alt="Lippincott CE login window with registration link">

5. Complete the profile form and select **Register**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-08.png" alt="Lippincott Professional Development registration form">

6. When registration is complete, select **Download Certificate**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-09.png" alt="Lippincott Download Certificate confirmation">

7. If prompted, sign in again to view the certificate.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-11.png" alt="Lippincott Professional Development sign-in page">

8. Open **Contact Hours** to view, print, or save the CE certificate.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-13.png" alt="Lippincott Contact Hours page with certificate option">

9. Return to the LMS course window and select **Exit Course**. The link may take a moment to activate while Lippincott and the LMS exchange completion data.

   <img class="guide-image guide-image--small" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-14.png" alt="Exit Course button in the LMS course window">

10. Confirm the course appears on the **Completed** transcript.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-15.png" alt="Completed Lippincott training on the LMS transcript">

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-lippincott-ce-certificate/step-16.png" alt="View Certificate option for completed Lippincott training">

## Notes

- Use the LMS certificate for Fairview completion documentation.
- Use the Lippincott certificate when continuing education credit documentation is required.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
