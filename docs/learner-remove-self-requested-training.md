# Remove Self-Requested Training

Use this guide to remove self-requested training from your transcript.

## Before You Begin

- Self-requested training is training you requested or launched yourself.
- Assigned training from a leader, administrator, or automatic assignment cannot usually be removed this way.
- You can remove training that is **Not Started** or **In Progress**. Completed training cannot be removed by the learner.

## Steps

1. Go to **My Transcript** from the home page or menu.
2. Find the training item.
3. Open the item dropdown and select **Remove**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-remove-self-requested-training/step-01.png" alt="Transcript item dropdown with Remove option">

4. Select a removal reason. If you choose **Other**, enter a comment.
5. Select **Submit**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-remove-self-requested-training/step-02.png" alt="Remove training reason and comment form">

## Notes

- If **Remove** is not available, the item may be assigned or already completed.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
