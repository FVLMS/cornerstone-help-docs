# Create a Video Lesson

Use this guide to create a video learning object that can stand alone or be added to a curriculum.

## Before You Begin

- Confirm whether the video will be uploaded as a file or embedded from YouTube.
- Supported uploaded formats include `.mpg`, `.mpeg`, `.wmv`, `.flv`, `.m4v`, and `.mp4`.
- Prepare the title, description, subject, owner information, and expected training hours before starting.

## Steps

1. Go to **Menu > Content Management > Catalog > Learning Objects > Videos**.
2. Select **Create a Video Course**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/content-create-video-lesson/step-01.png" alt="Videos page with Create a Video Course button">

3. Choose how the video will be added:
   - **Upload a New Video**: attach the video file and wait for upload processing.
   - **Embed a Video**: paste the YouTube URL and select **Embed**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/content-create-video-lesson/step-02.png" alt="Upload a New Video option">

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/content-create-video-lesson/step-03.png" alt="Embed a Video option with URL field">

4. Preview the video if the preview option is available.
5. Select **Next** to open **General Information**.
6. Complete the required and recommended fields:
   - **Title**
   - **Provider**: use **Fairview** for uploaded videos or **YouTube** for embedded videos.
   - **Training Hours**: enter the video length.
   - **Description**
   - **Credits**, if contact hours are offered.
   - **Subjects**
   - **Objectives**
   - **Audience Description**
   - **Owner Name** and **Owner Email**
7. Confirm the video details and select **Finish**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/content-create-video-lesson/step-04.png" alt="Video lesson confirmation page">

## Notes

- After creation, update availability in the catalog before learners should access the video.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
