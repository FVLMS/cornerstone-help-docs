# Edit Due Dates

Use this guide to change the due date for a training item on a learner's transcript.

## Before You Begin

- Confirm you have the right learner and the right training item.
- This permission may be available to managers, educators, and administrators depending on role setup.
- Due date changes affect the learner's transcript and overdue status.

## Steps

1. Search for the learner.
2. Open the learner's transcript.
3. Find the training item and select **View Training Details**. If the button is hidden, open the item's dropdown menu.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/admin-edit-due-dates/step-01.png" alt="Transcript item with View Training Details option">

4. Select **Edit** next to **Due Date**.
5. Enter the new due date.
6. Select **Save**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/admin-edit-due-dates/step-02.png" alt="Due Date edit window with Save button">

## Notes

- If the due date is part of a required assignment rule, confirm the change should be made manually before saving.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
