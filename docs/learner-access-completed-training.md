# Access Previously Completed Training

Use this guide to find and reopen training you already completed in Cornerstone.

## Steps

1. From the LMS home page, go to **My Transcript**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-access-completed-training/step-01.png" alt="My Transcript link from the learner home page">

2. Use **Training Status** to filter to **Completed**, or search by keyword to find the training item.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-access-completed-training/step-02.png" alt="Transcript page filtered to completed training">

3. Open the dropdown next to **View Certificate** and select **Launch**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-access-completed-training/step-03.png" alt="Completed training dropdown with Launch option">

## Notes

- Some completed items can be relaunched for review but cannot be retaken from the completed transcript.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
