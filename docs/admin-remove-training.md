# Remove Training

Use this guide to remove a self-enrolled or self-requested training item from a transcript.

## Before You Begin

- Confirm the training should be removed instead of completed, withdrawn, or left on the transcript.
- Be careful with required or assigned training. Removing the item may not remove the underlying assignment rule.
- Add a clear reason and comment so the transcript history is understandable later.

## Steps

1. Open the learner's transcript.
2. Find the training item.
3. Open the item dropdown menu and select **Remove**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/admin-remove-training/step-01.png" alt="Transcript item dropdown with Remove option">

4. Enter the reason and comments.
5. Select **Submit**.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/admin-remove-training/step-02.png" alt="Remove training confirmation form">

## Notes

- If the training reappears after removal, it may be controlled by an assignment rule. Contact the LMS team before repeated removals.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
