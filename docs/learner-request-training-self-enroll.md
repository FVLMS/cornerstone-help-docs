# Request Training or Self-Enroll

Use this guide to find a learning item in the catalog and add it to your transcript.

## Steps

1. Search for the learning item from **Catalog** or the global search box. Use a keyword or part of the lesson title.

   <img class="guide-image guide-image--small" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-request-training-self-enroll/step-01.png" alt="Catalog button on the learner home page">

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-request-training-self-enroll/step-02.png" alt="Catalog search results for a learning item">

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-request-training-self-enroll/step-03.png" alt="Global search field for training">

2. Open the desired item.
3. On **Training Details**, select **Launch** to open the training and add it to your transcript.

   <img class="guide-image guide-image--small" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/learner-request-training-self-enroll/step-04.png" alt="Training Details page with Launch button">

## Remove Self-Enrolled Training

1. On your transcript, open the dropdown next to **Launch**.
2. Select **Remove**.
3. Choose a reason, add a comment if needed, and select **Submit**.

## Notes

- Self-enrolled learning usually does not have a due date.
- Assigned training may not be removable by the learner.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
