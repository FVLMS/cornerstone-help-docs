# Completing an Evaluation

Some trainings on Cornerstone have required evaluations the must be completed before the training itself will be marked complete. This guide will demonstrate how to access and complete evaluations.

## Accessing Evaluations

Pending evaluations can be accessed one of two ways. First, on the Cornerstone home page there is a widget at the bottom of the page that contains pending evaluations:

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation1.png" alt="">

Second, you can find the training on your transcript page, where you will see the “Evaluate” button:

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation2.png" alt="">

## Completing an Evaluation

After opening the evaluation, answer the questions and then click submit. Once the evaluation is submitted, then the training will be marked complete:

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation3.png" alt="">
