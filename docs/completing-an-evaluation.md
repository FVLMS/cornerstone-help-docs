# Completing an Evaluation

Some Cornerstone training has a required evaluation that must be completed before the training is marked complete. Use this guide to access and complete an evaluation.

## Accessing Evaluations

You can access pending evaluations in two ways. First, use the pending evaluations widget at the bottom of the Cornerstone home page.

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation1.png" alt="Pending evaluations widget on the Cornerstone home page">

Second, find the training on your transcript and select **Evaluate**.

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation2.png" alt="Evaluate button for a training item on the transcript">

## Completing an Evaluation

After opening the evaluation, answer the questions and select **Submit**. The training will be marked complete after you submit the evaluation.

<img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/completing-an-evaluation/Evaluation3.png" alt="Evaluation questions and Submit button">
