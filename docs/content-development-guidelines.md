# Content Development Guidelines

Use these standards when creating or updating Cornerstone learning objects for Fairview.

## General Standards

- **Test in Pilot first.** Build and test new or revised content in Pilot before loading it into Production.
- **Search before creating.** Check for existing materials, tests, curricula, and sessions before creating a new object.
- **Use clear titles.** Avoid internal codes or modifiers unless they are needed for the learner or administrator to identify the item.
- **Use Player 2.0.** Select Player 2.0 when that setting is available.
- **Use reversioning for updates.** Reversion existing learning objects instead of copying and replacing them.
- **Notify the LMS team before reversioning auto-assigned curricula.** Reversioning can break auto-assignment rules if the LMS team does not update the assignment setup.

## Material Standards

- Do not create mandatory training on your own. Work with the LMS team for mandatory items.
- Load documents as PDFs unless the LMS team approves another format.
- Recurrence is off by default. Enable recurrence only when the process calls for it.
- Electronic signature may be on by default. Turn it off when learners only need to review the material and mark it complete.

## Notes

- When in doubt, ask before publishing learner-facing content in Production.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
