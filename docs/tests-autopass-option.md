# Use the Test AutoPass Option

Use this guide to mark a free form essay test for AutoPass processing.

## Before You Begin

- AutoPass is intended for free form essay tests that should be completed by the LMS team after learner submission.
- Learners still move to **Pending Grading** when they submit the test.
- The LMS team marks AutoPass tests **Complete** at 100% during the next business-day process.

## Steps

1. After creating or updating the free form essay test, go to **Menu > Content Management > Catalog > Course Catalog**.
2. Search for the test title.
3. In the **Actions** column, open the ellipses menu and select **Edit**.

   <img class="guide-image guide-image--small" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/tests-autopass-option/step-02.png" alt="Course Catalog Actions menu with Edit option">

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/tests-autopass-option/step-03.png" alt="Course Catalog search results for the test">

4. On the **General** tab, scroll to the bottom of the page.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/tests-autopass-option/step-04.png" alt="Test edit General tab">

5. Check **Auto-Pass**.

   <img class="guide-image guide-image--small" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/tests-autopass-option/step-05.png" alt="Auto-Pass checkbox selected">

6. Select **Save**.

## Notes

- Do not use AutoPass when a grader needs to evaluate learner responses manually.
- Contact the LMS team at LMS@fairview.org if you have questions about this process.
