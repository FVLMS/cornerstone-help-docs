# New Reporting Page

Reporting 2.0 has been redesigned and enhanced. You can now quickly view recently accessed reports and you have more available filters, more options available to customize your view, and an easier time accessing your most commonly used reports.

## Enabling the New Reporting Page

1. To enable the new Reporting 2.0, navigate to the Reporting 2.0 page and click the button in the top right.
2. You can also disable the new Reporting 2.0 view if you change your mind.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/new-reporting-page/Reporting1.png" alt="">

## Navigating the Page

1. You can view your most recently accessed reports.
2. You can view your folders and folders that have been shared with you.
3. You can change from a card view to list view.
4. You can progress through the pages at the bottom.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/new-reporting-page/Reporting2.png" alt="">

## Finding Your Reports

1. A search bar can be found at the top.
2. Reports can be favorited by clicking the star.
3. You can filter with the buttons at the top.

   Note that since most users cannot create reports, only the Favorites and All Reports tabs will be useful.
4. You can change the sorting from Last Accessed to Alphabetical. Note that most all reports will be in the Learning category, so the Categories filter will not be useful.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/new-reporting-page/Reporting3.png" alt="">

## Exporting Your Reports

1. To export a report, click the action button and select "Refresh & Download."
2. If you need a different file type, click the action button, then click "Refresh." Then, in the "Download" option, select the desired file type.

   <img class="guide-image" src="https://mnfhs.sharepoint.com/sites/LearningManagementSystem/Shared%20Documents/cornerstone-help-docs/new-reporting-page/Reporting4.png" alt="">

## List View

The list view contains all the same information as the card view, but you may prefer it for two reasons:

1. It is simpler to sort by name or access date.
2. It is simpler to export reports.
